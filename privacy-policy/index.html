Privacy Policy

RezFlow (Engineered by Automania)

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RezFlow, a property technology platform engineered and operated by Automania ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal data when you interact with the RezFlow Student Portal and our automated communication channels, including our WhatsApp notifications.

By using our platform, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. We strictly adhere to the provisions of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa and international data privacy best practices.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with seamless accommodation management and security services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, student number, and contact details (including mobile phone numbers used for WhatsApp communications).
  • Accommodation Data: Room or unit numbers allocated to you within the property.
  • Operational Data: Maintenance requests, ticket logs, and snag reports submitted via the portal.
  • Security Data: Visitor access logs and gate PIN generation requests.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively to facilitate property operations and improve your living experience. Specifically, your data is used to:

  • Process, manage, and update you on maintenance and snagging tickets.
  • Facilitate secure visitor access and gate entry logging.
  • Send highly relevant, time-sensitive operational alerts and transactional updates (e.g., ticket resolution, security PINs, emergency property notices).
  • WhatsApp Messaging: We use your phone number to send automated, template-based utility messages via the WhatsApp Business API regarding your active requests. We do not use your phone number to send marketing or promotional material via WhatsApp.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share necessary data with trusted third-party infrastructure providers to operate the RezFlow platform securely:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Business API): Used strictly to route and deliver automated utility notifications to your WhatsApp account. By opting in, you acknowledge that messages are securely routed through Meta's cloud infrastructure subject to Meta's data policies.
  • Cloud Infrastructure (e.g., Supabase): Used for secure, encrypted backend database hosting and data storage.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ strict technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All backend databases are secured, and API data transfers are encrypted. We retain your personal information only for as long as you remain a resident at the managed property or as required by operational and legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction or deletion of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Opt-Out: Withdraw your consent to receive automated WhatsApp notifications at any time by updating your preferences in the RezFlow portal or contacting property management directly.
