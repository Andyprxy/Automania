Case Study: Farm Mania

The Agent That
Never Sleeps.

See how we transformed a local poultry business's chaotic WhatsApp messages into a streamlined, 24/7 automated sales machine.

The Bottleneck

Farm Mania produces premium chickens, but they faced a critical issue: Customers order when the owner is asleep.

Answering "How much is a full chicken?" 50 times a day was draining time, and slow replies meant lost revenue. They needed a way to capture sales instantly, day or night.

The Solution

We deployed an AI-powered Sales Agent on WhatsApp. It doesn't just chat; it's integrated with their inventory database. It can quote prices, check stock, and confirm orders automatically.
